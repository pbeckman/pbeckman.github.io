COURSE DESCRIPTION

This course provides a rigorous introduction to the study of ordinary differential equations, linear algebra, and a brief introduction into partial differential equations. Students will learn the basic techniques for understanding and solving differential equations that arise in applications.

MATERIALS

Textbook: Martin Braun, Differential Equations and Their Applications, 4th. Ed
